Look & Learn Classes

Look & Learn classes are a chance to spend the day with me alongside an intimate group of passionate stylists, watching my process as I create one of my signature techniques from start to finish.

Each class will focus on one model, giving you the opportunity to see how I approach the consultation, technique, formulations, and finishing touches that bring the final result together. You'll see how I build a personalised colour and the small details that help create a result that feels intentional.

We'll also chat about building your dream clientele, creating content that connects with the clients you want to attract, and showing up on socials.

With plenty of time for questions and conversations throughout the day, my aim is for you to leave feeling inspired, with fresh ideas and techniques you can adapt behind the chair and make your own.
