Frequently Asked Questions

Who are Shadow Days best suited to?

Shadow Days are perfect for stylists who prefer an intimate education day and want an honest insight into the day-to-day life of another stylist's approach. They may also have questions they don't feel confident asking in a class setting.

Who are Look & Learn classes best suited to?

Look & Learn classes are perfect for stylists who learn best from a more structured class environment. Stylists who enjoy learning alongside others & bouncing off them throughout the day.

Who is a 1:1 best suited to?

1:1 education is tailored towards stylists wanting to focus on one specific technique & feel they'd benefit from individual support. They want real time feedback & guidance in an intimate setting.

Who is a private class best suited to?

Private classes are perfect for salons who want tailored education, without the need to travel & are great for salon owners wanting to invest in the whole team.
