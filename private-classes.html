Private Classes

Investment upon request based on attendees & location

Private classes are a great option for salons looking for tailored education, in the comfort of their own space.

Held at your salon, I'll come and deliver a look-and-learn session for you and your team, focused on the techniques and topics most relevant to you.

During the session, I'll demonstrate my approach step by step, explaining my thought process, formulations & placement of your chosen technique, with plenty of opportunity to ask questions throughout.

These sessions are designed to be relaxed, informative, and completely personalised to the needs of your team.
